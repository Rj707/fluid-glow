## 1. What Does the App Actually Do?

At its core, **Fluid Glow** provides an instant digital sanctuary for stress relief, creative play, and sensory satisfaction:

1. **Touch to Flow:** As soon as you place one or more fingers on the pure-black screen, vibrant, glowing liquid streams erupt under your fingertips.
2. **Dynamic Physics:** Swirling, dragging, and flicking creates realistic whirlpools, vortex collisions, liquid drag, and organic dissipation that look like luminous silk or glowing cosmic plasma.
3. **Harmonic Soundscapes:** As your fingers accelerate and create swirls, an onboard synthesizer generates calming musical chimes whose pitch and tone shift according to finger speed and velocity.
4. **Haptic Resonance:** Micro-vibrations pulse through the device, letting you physically "feel" the liquid current rushing across the glass.
5. **Freeze & Save Art:** Whenever you create a swirl pattern you love, a single tap freezes time, triggers a camera flash animation, and exports a high-definition 4K wallpaper directly into your device's photo gallery.

## 3. Core Feature Breakdown

### A. Hyper-Responsive Fluid Dynamics Simulation
* **Silky 60 / 120 FPS Rendering:** Optimized for modern high-refresh-rate displays (Apple ProMotion 120Hz on iPhone/iPad and 120Hz AMOLED on Android).
* **Realistic Navier-Stokes-inspired Particle Mechanics:** Accurately simulates fluid viscosity, momentum conservation, rotational curl, velocity damping, and smooth particle dissipation.
* **Particle Cap & Battery Efficiency:** Intelligently caps active particle counts (~1,200 particles) to maintain locked 60/120 FPS performance with zero frame drops and minimal battery consumption.

### B. Generative ASMR Harmonic Audio Engine
* **Real-Time Synthesizer:** Rather than looping static MP3 audio tracks, the app features an onboard procedural synthesizer (`ASMRAudioEngine` on iOS, `ToneSynth` on Android).
* **Velocity-Responsive Pitch:** Swirling slowly produces deep, warm, grounding bass tones; fast flicks and multi-finger acceleration generate bright, resonant celestial chimes.
* **Ad-Aware Audio Ducking:** Sound gracefully fades out during system interruptions or video ads, and resumes smoothly afterward.
* **Sensory Toggle:** Can be muted at any time from the Settings menu.

### C. Tactile Micro-Haptic Feedback
* **Apple Taptic Engine & Android Haptic Motors:** Provides subtle, crisp physical pulses as fingers navigate across the canvas.
* **Fluid Wave Feedback:** Micro-vibrations intensify when colliding multiple vortexes or capturing wallpapers, creating physical immersion that makes the screen feel like a pool of glowing water.

### E. One-Tap 4K OLED Wallpaper Snapshot
* **Instant Capture:** Tapping the shutter button captures the exact instantaneous state of the particles on canvas.
* **Procedural Fullscreen Fallback:** If the user taps the camera on a black canvas, the engine automatically synthesizes an ultra-dense, full-screen radiant aurora swirl so no saved wallpaper is ever blank.
* **Camera Flash & Toast Notification:** Provides a satisfying high-key flash effect, strong haptic pulse, and a floating glassmorphic pill (*"Wallpaper Saved to Photos"*).
* **Direct Gallery Integration:** Saves directly to Apple Photos (iOS) or Android MediaStore/Pictures (`Pictures/FluidGlow`) with zero manual file export required.

### F. Gesture Masterclass & Multi-Touch Controls
The app features an interactive guide that teaches users distinct fluid gestures:

1. **Single-Finger Glide:** Draws graceful, silky trails and ribbons that drift and curl naturally.
2. **Dual-Finger Spiral:** Creates counter-rotating vortexes that attract and swirl together into a fluid whirlpool.
3. **Multi-Touch Burst (3 to 5 fingers):** Simultaneous multi-touch points trigger explosive turbulence and particle collisions.
4. **Hold & Dwell:** Holding a finger still anchors a gravity vortex, drawing nearby fluid into a dense, glowing orb.
5. **Double Tap Canvas:** Instantly clears all particles for a clean slate.

## 6. Privacy, Offline Access & Technical Architecture

### Privacy-First Guarantee:
* **Zero Personal Data Collection:** No accounts, no email sign-ups, no tracking of personal files.
* **No Image Uploads:** Wallpapers created by the user are saved strictly to the local device gallery and are never uploaded to any cloud server.
* **No Microphone Required:** ASMR audio is 100% synthesized mathematically on-device; no audio is ever recorded.

### 100% Offline Capability:
* The entire fluid simulation, procedural sound synthesizer, haptic feedback, and wallpaper generator function flawlessly in Airplane Mode without an active internet connection.

### Cross-Platform Architecture:
* **iOS App:** Written in native Swift and SwiftUI, leveraging Metal/CoreGraphics rendering, Apple Taptic Engine (`UIImpactFeedbackGenerator`), StoreKit 2 in-app purchases, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for GDPR consent.
* **Android App:** Written in native Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, featuring custom Canvas physics, software tone synthesis (`ToneSynth`), Google Play Billing, and Android scoped storage (`MediaStore`).
